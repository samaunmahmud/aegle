### `eeg-seizure` v0.2.0

**Held-out test (28965 cases):** window AUC 0.750; 36.6% of 41 seizures detected, median delay 52.0 s, 2.27 false alarms/hour over 32.2 h.

*Data:* CHB-MIT Scalp EEG (Shoeb, 2009; ODC-BY 1.0): recordings with seizures from 23 children with intractable epilepsy, Boston Children's Hospital

*Caveat:* Paediatric, one centre, patients with intractable epilepsy; evaluated on recordings that contain seizures only. With the alarm rule held to about 2 false alarms per hour it finds only about a third of seizures in unseen patients: it cannot rule seizures out; do not use for clinical decisions.
